# Interview guide

## Before the interview

Consent phrase (say it at the start):

> «Мы студенты, делаем дипломный проект — систему онлайн-записи к врачу. Разговор займёт 10–15 минут. Можно задать вам несколько вопросов о том, как вы записываетесь к врачу? Мы не будем указывать ваше имя. Можно ли записать разговор только для наших заметок?»

Record: role, age group, date, format (phone / video / in person). Do not record the name, phone number or IIN.

## Questions for patients

1. How did you book an appointment with a doctor the last time? Which service or channel did you use?
2. How long did it take to find a suitable time? What was difficult?
3. Did you look at several doctors or clinics to find an earlier time? Was the distance to the clinic important?
4. Have you received a referral to a specialist or for tests? How did you book after that? How many visits did it take?
5. Have you ever missed an appointment or forgotten about it? Why? Did you receive a reminder?
6. Have you booked for a child or a parent? How convenient was it?
7. If you could change one thing in how booking works today, what would it be?

## Questions for doctors and registry staff

1. How do patients usually book with you? What problems do you see most often?
2. How often do patients not come? What happens to the freed time?
3. How are referrals issued and checked today? Do patients come without a valid referral?
4. Have you seen double bookings or schedule conflicts?
5. What information about the patient do you need before the visit?
6. What takes the most time in scheduling or registration?
